December 2014. If the world were static, we could have monotonically increasing confidence in our beliefs. The more varied experience a belief survived, the less likely it would be false. Most people implicitly believe something like this about their opinions. And they are justified in doing so with opinions about things that do not change much, like human nature. But you cannot trust your opinions in the same way about things that change, which could include practically everything else.

When experts are wrong, it is often because they are experts on an earlier version of the world. Is it possible to avoid that? Can you protect yourself against obsolete beliefs? To some extent, yes. I spent almost a decade investing in early stage startups, and protecting yourself against obsolete beliefs is exactly what you have to do to succeed as a startup investor.
