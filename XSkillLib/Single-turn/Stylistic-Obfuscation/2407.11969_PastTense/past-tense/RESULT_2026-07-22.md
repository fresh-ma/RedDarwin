isolates the attack's contribution: it bypasses almost nothing (rule 0.10, content 0.00), while the
past-tense rewrite lifts refusal-bypass 6× (0.10 → 0.60) and yields genuine operational content on 2/20.

## Verdict

**faithful-success (reduced-scale).** The past-tense mechanism reproduces cleanly: the past > future >
present ordering holds on all three judges, past-tense lifts refusal-bypass 6× over the direct baseline,
and 2/20 past-tense attacks yield genuinely operational harmful content on an aligned model. The audited
ASR (0.10) sits far below the paper's 0.88 headline because this run uses **1 restart vs the paper's 20**
and substitutes deepseek-v4-flash for GPT-4o; the 88% is a best-of-20-restarts number and is not
comparable to a single-restart pass. Mechanism reproduced; the magnitude gap is a
configuration/target-substitution artifact, not a fidelity failure. A 100-row × 20-restart run would be
needed for a direct magnitude comparison.
